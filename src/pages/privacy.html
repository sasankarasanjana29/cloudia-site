Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Cloudia is an iPhone app and this website, both made by one independent developer, Sasanka Rasanjana (“we” on this page). This page explains what each of them keeps, where it is kept, and how you remove it. The short version: your loops live on your phone, nothing is tracked, and nothing is sold.

1. This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies: no web fonts, no scripts and no embedded media. It does not ask for any information about you.

Like every website, it is delivered by a hosting provider, which may record standard technical details of each visit (such as your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time) to deliver the site and keep it secure. Those records are kept by the host under its own policy, and we do not use them to identify you.

2. What stays on your phone

Everything you add to Cloudia: what each loop is, who it is with, amounts, dates and categories, plus your settings and your answers to the questions asked when you first open the app.

All of it is stored only on your device. It is not sent anywhere unless you turn on sync.

3. If you turn on sync

Sync is optional. If you create an account, Cloudia stores your email address and a copy of your loops with Supabase, a hosting provider that keeps the data on our behalf, so your loops can follow you to another device.

  • Your password goes straight to Supabase, which stores it in scrambled (hashed) form. We never see it.
  • Your synced loops can only be read by your own signed-in account.
  • Supabase may store data in a different country from yours. It does so under its own data protection terms.
  • We use this data only to provide sync. The legal basis, where one is needed, is providing the service you asked for.

4. Reminders

The current version of Cloudia does not send notifications. If reminders come back, they will be scheduled on your phone by iOS, not sent through any server, and this page will say so first.

5. What we never do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or renting of your data, and no sharing with anyone except the hosting providers named above.

6. Other people in your loops

Loops often name other people, like a friend who owes you money. Cloudia only stores those names for you. Please keep what you write about others fair and lawful, and leave out sensitive details you would not want stored.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your phone, data stays until you remove it. Profile, then Delete everything, erases it, and so does deleting the app.
  • Synced data stays until you delete your account. Profile, then Sync across devices, then Delete account, removes your account and everything synced to it straight away. The hosting provider may keep encrypted backups for a short time before they expire.
  • Profile, then Export your loops, gives you a copy of your data at any time.

8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can see, copy, correct and delete your data, and object to how it is used. Most of this you can do yourself inside the app. These rights come from laws such as the GDPR in the European Union and the United Kingdom, the CCPA in California and the Personal Data Protection Act in Sri Lanka, and we give them to everyone.

You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

9. Security

Synced data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and your sign-in session is kept in the iOS Keychain. No method is perfect, so we only keep what the app needs.

10. Children

Cloudia is not made for children. Please do not create an account if you are under 13, or under 16 where your local law sets that age.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here and in the app, with a new date at the top. A change that affects your data will be pointed out in the app before it applies.

12. Contact

A contact address will be published here before Cloudia is offered to the public. Until then, everything on this page can be done yourself inside the app.
