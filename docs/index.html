Where do your loose ends live right now?

For most people: in their head, a notes app, or a screenshot they will never open again. Cloudia gives every one of them a place and a date.

In my head
Screenshots
Notes app
Email inbox

Cloudia holds what is still in motion, so you can stop carrying it in your head.

Two kinds of loops. One place for both.

Add it by voice

Tap Just say it and tell Cloudia the way you would tell a friend. She works out whose turn it is, puts what, who, when and how much on their own cards to check, and one tap adds it.

Or add it by hand

Pick My turn for something you promised, or Waiting for something someone owes you. Add who it is with, an amount if there is one, and a day.

See what needs you today

Today puts what is due first and what is late in red. The week strip marks every day: red for late, blue for due, green for done.

Close it with a seal

Tap It arrived or Mark as done, and Cloudia climbs onto the card and presses a green wax seal into it. Your phone taps along with her.

Streaming app

Your loops stay on your phone.

Cloudia works fully without an account. Sync is there if you want the same loops on another device, and you can take your data out, or delete it, whenever you like.

  • No ads. None, and none planned.
  • No tracking. No analytics in the app or on this site, and no cookies.
  • Yours to take. Export to a spreadsheet, or back up to a file.
  • Yours to delete. Everything on the phone, or your whole account, from inside the app.

Questions

Is Cloudia free?

Yes. Cloudia is free, including 20 of its 50 categories. A Cloudia Pro plan may come later. Nothing is for sale today.

When can I get it?

Cloudia is being built now and is coming to iPhone. There is no release date yet.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Cloudia is made for iPhone first.

Can I add a loop by talking?

Yes, in English for now. Tap Just say it, or press and hold the + button, and say it as you would to a friend: "Anna owes me $60 for the concert tickets by Friday." Cloudia shows each part on its own card to check before you add it. Your iPhone turns the speech into text itself; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Will Cloudia remind me?

Not yet. Reminders are being rebuilt. For now, Today shows what is due and what is late, and the week strip marks every day.

Where are my loops stored?

On your phone. If you turn on sync, a copy is kept with Supabase, the hosting provider Cloudia uses, so your loops can follow you to another device. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I delete everything?

Yes. In Profile, Delete everything erases the loops on your phone, and Delete account removes your synced account and everything in it.

Who makes Cloudia?

One person, Sasanka Rasanjana, as an independent project.
